Short answer

SEO cost depends on competition, website quality, content gaps, and how fast you want to move.

A small service business may need keyword research, page fixes, and local SEO. A competitive ecommerce or real estate brand may need deeper content, technical cleanup, category pages, reporting, and continuous optimization.

WTB structures SEO around clear deliverables so you know what the money is paying for: audit, keyword map, page optimization, content plan, local signals, technical recommendations, and reporting.

Buyer questions

What should you ask before paying for SEO?

Will SEO bring instant sales?

No serious SEO expert should promise instant ranking. SEO builds visibility over time, but the right pages can improve trust, discovery, and lead quality.

Do we need content every month?

Usually yes. Search engines need helpful pages that answer what buyers are asking. The right content depends on your niche, competition, and service offer.

Can WTB also fix website issues?

Yes. If the website structure, speed, copy, forms, or landing pages are hurting SEO, WTB can recommend or implement improvements.
