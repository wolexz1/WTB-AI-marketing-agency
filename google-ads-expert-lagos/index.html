Questions

Common questions before we start.

Do I need a website before running Google Ads?

A strong landing page or website is strongly recommended because ads need somewhere credible and conversion-ready to send traffic.

Can WTB set up tracking?

Yes. WTB can support Google Analytics, Google Search Console, conversion tracking, and reporting setup.

Which Google Ads type should I start with?

It depends on your offer. Search Ads are often best for direct buyer intent, while YouTube, Display, Performance Max, and Demand Gen can support awareness and retargeting.
