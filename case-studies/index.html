Featured work

Client stories built around outcomes, not activity.

Each project used a different mix of strategy, paid media, SEO, UGC, events, partnerships, website optimization, and campaign execution.

Food retail

Cold Stone Creamery

WTB created foot-traffic campaigns built around social moments people could join. Ladies' Night brought groups into stores, while the Brain Freeze Challenge made ice cream buying feel playful, competitive, and shareable.

  • Ladies' Night activation for store visits
  • Brain Freeze Challenge for participation and sales
  • Experience-led campaign ideas for nationwide attention
"The ideas felt bold at first, but they knew what would get people into the stores and make the product fun again."
Food, UGC and paid media

Domino's Pizza

WTB supported a high-engagement campaign mix including a Showmax partnership, influencer UGC, Google Display, YouTube, Meta, Facebook, and Instagram ads. The offer gave people a reason to walk into stores and buy.

  • Showmax partnership campaign
  • Influencer and UGC content angles
  • YouTube, Display, Meta and Instagram ad support
"They kept the campaign practical. People could see the offer, walk in, and act on it."
Skincare ecommerce

BuyBetter

For BuyBetter, WTB supported ecommerce visibility with SEO, Google Display campaigns, content strategy, and product-led messaging. The work helped skincare and cosmetic products become easier to discover, understand, and buy.

  • SEO ranking work across product pages
  • Google Display campaign support
  • Social media content strategy for ecommerce growth
"They brought structure to the ecommerce side and helped the products become easier to find, understand, and buy."
Legal SEO

Olisa Agbakoba Legal

WTB focused on search visibility for high-value legal sectors including sports, entertainment, space, maritime, and leading law-firm searches in Lagos. The goal was to connect authority-led legal content with real search demand.

  • SEO across specialist legal sectors
  • Ranking support for high-intent Lagos law-firm searches
  • Stronger enquiry path from organic visibility
"We pushed back at the start, but they understood the legal sectors and stayed consistent until better enquiries started coming in."
Ecommerce platform

Jollof.com

WTB helped shape a fresh ecommerce platform from early product direction into a clearer user experience. The work included feature thinking, vendor acquisition support, product guidance, and growth direction.

  • Feature and product experience guidance
  • Vendor and product acquisition support
  • User journey optimization for ecommerce adoption
"They challenged the product flow in a useful way and helped make the platform clearer for users and vendors."
Education advertising

GCS Education

WTB used Meta and Google advertising to support study-abroad recruitment. In one calendar year, the campaigns helped move almost 150 students toward Canada, the UK, and the US.

  • Meta Ads for student recruitment
  • Google Ads for high-intent education searches
  • Study-abroad lead generation across major destinations
"They cared about enquiry quality, not just lead numbers. That made the student recruitment campaigns stronger."
UGC and product demand

Pinkberry

WTB supported product awareness and sales campaigns using UGC, Meta ads, and Google Display. The work helped make yogurt products more visible, more desirable, and easier for buyers to act on.

  • UGC-led product storytelling
  • Meta Ads for demand and recall
  • Google Display campaigns for product visibility
"They understood that the product had to look desirable first. The content and ads made that easier."
