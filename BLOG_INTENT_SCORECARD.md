# WTB Blog Intent Scorecard

This scorecard ranks current WTB blog articles by:

- `Search intent strength`: how strong the query intent is
- `Conversion potential`: how likely the topic is to lead naturally to inquiry, brief, call, or service comparison
- `Role`: whether the article is best for direct conversion, assisted conversion, or broader authority

Use this sheet when deciding:

- what to promote first
- what to expand into topic clusters
- what to connect more tightly to service pages
- what to refresh with newer data later

## Scoring Key

- `High`: strong buyer or problem-solving intent
- `Medium`: useful intent, but usually one step further from inquiry
- `Assist`: supports trust, education, or early-stage awareness more than direct conversion
