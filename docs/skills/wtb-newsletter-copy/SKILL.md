# WTB Newsletter Copy

Write for Nigerian business owners and marketers who want useful AI, business, and marketing ideas. The goal of a blog-led email is a qualified click to one article, not a complete summary of it.

## Choose the angle

- Use Wole's topic for this edition when he provides one. Otherwise, select a live WTB blog post, read it, verify the destination URL, and avoid recently featured posts.
- Find one specific reader tension in the article: lost time, missed customers, weak marketing, confusing AI advice, or an overlooked opportunity. Ground the hook in what the article actually resolves.
- Draft at least three distinct subject angles internally; present only the strongest unless Wole requests options. Pair it with preview text that adds information instead of repeating the subject.

## Write the email

- Keep the body roughly 80-130 words unless the topic needs less. Open with a concrete moment, pointed observation, or useful question. Move quickly from tension to the insight the article offers.
- Sound like a sharp, warm WTB human: energetic, conversational, occasionally witty, and Nigeria-aware. Light observational humor is welcome when it fits. Avoid generic AI hype, forced slang, clichés, and long setup.
- Give the reader enough context to trust the click, but leave the article's useful detail to discover. Use one primary action, linked directly to the verified article, with specific benefit-led anchor text.
- Make subject, preview, body, and article deliver the same promise. Never invent results, client stories, statistics, scarcity, or urgency. Do not use deceptive `Re:`/`Fwd:` framing, shame, or fear as a substitute for value.

## Review before approval

- Read the draft aloud; cut any sentence that does not sharpen the hook, clarify the benefit, or move the reader to the article. Ensure the first screen works on mobile.
- Check every claim against the linked post and every link against the live destination. Preserve the branded template, social icons, and unsubscribe option.
- Present the exact subject, preview text, body, link, and proposed send time to Wole. Do not schedule or send until he explicitly approves that exact edition.
- After a send, use Kit's report to learn from article-link clicks, click rate, unsubscribes, and complaints. Treat opens as directional because privacy features can distort them. Change one meaningful angle at a time rather than chasing vanity metrics.
