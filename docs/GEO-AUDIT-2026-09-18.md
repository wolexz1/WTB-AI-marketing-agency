# WTB GEO and AI-search audit

**Reviewed:** September 18, 2026  
**Scope:** Public source, crawl controls, entity clarity, structured data, internal service descriptions and AI-search readiness.

## Result

WTB has a strong technical starting point for AI-assisted discovery. The site permits Google, Bing, ChatGPT Search, Perplexity and Claude crawlers; provides a sitemap; uses canonical URLs; and already has a consistent organisation identity, contact details, social profiles, service descriptions, local relevance and third-party coverage on the homepage.

This does not guarantee a mention or citation in any AI product. AI systems choose answers dynamically. The work below improves the conditions for WTB to be crawled, understood, checked and cited accurately.

## Implemented in this pass

- Confirmed explicit access for `OAI-SearchBot`, `ChatGPT-User`, `GPTBot`, `PerplexityBot`, `ClaudeBot`, `Google-Extended` and `Bingbot` in `robots.txt`.
- Confirmed the public sitemap and `llms.txt` discovery document are present.
- Confirmed organisation identity, services, Nigeria/Lagos/Abuja coverage, contact details, social profiles and externally linked media coverage on the homepage.
- Added visible direct answers and matching FAQ/Breadcrumb schema to the About and Contact trust pages.
- Kept schema limited to information visitors can also read on the page.

## What WTB should keep doing

1. Publish original Nigeria-specific buyer guides, comparisons, pricing explanations, first-party campaign lessons and useful checklists.
2. Make every core service page answer: what it is, who it is for, where WTB works, how the process works, what evidence supports it and the next action.
3. Keep the same name, phone, email, URLs and social profiles consistent across the website and verified external profiles.
4. Earn relevant independent mentions and links. Better third-party coverage is more durable than adding more hidden website signals.
5. Use Google Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools and GA4 to review AI-search referrals, branded queries, crawl/index status and lead quality monthly.

## Outside the website source

The following cannot be created truthfully from a website code change and should remain an operating task:

- Maintain a complete, accurate Google Business Profile if WTB has a customer-facing eligible location/service-area setup.
- Verify the domain in Bing Webmaster Tools and submit the sitemap.
- Keep social profiles, business directories and PR mentions factually consistent with the website.
- Publish real case evidence, client permissions, bylines and outcomes only where they can be substantiated.
- Track whether ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Copilot actually cite or refer traffic to WTB; no technical tag can force that outcome.
