## Competitor backlink evidence

OpenSEO backlink profiles checked on 2026-08-24 for three Nigerian agency competitors surfaced these relevant patterns:

- Socialander: Vanguard, Guardian Nigeria, BusinessDay, Intense.ng, Professions.ng, and agency comparison pages link to the company through founder stories, business commentary, and editorial lists.
- CKDigital: Disrupt Africa, IT News Africa, Tekedia, NaijaTechGuide, SkytechNG, BusinessYield, and Nigerian web/technology publications link through startup, payments, SEO, and agency explainers.
- Wild Fusion: the current profile returned only two live links, including a Lagos marketing comparison article and a Social Media Week Lagos page. It is not a strong prospect-source pattern on its own.

This is competitor evidence, not a guarantee that any publisher will link to WTB. The actionable overlap is the editorial format: original Nigerian business insight, founder education, technology explainers, and useful cost or implementation guides. Directory and obvious link-farm domains were excluded.

## Execution rules

1. Confirm the publication's current editorial route immediately before sending.
2. Pitch one useful story, not a request for a random backlink.
3. Offer a non-promotional article, expert comment, data point, or resource inclusion.
4. Use one relevant WTB link only when it helps the reader.
5. Send no more than 10-15 personalised pitches per week.
6. Follow up once after 5-7 days, then stop.
7. Record response, placement URL, target URL, link type, anchor, and referral traffic.

## Quality gate

Reject prospects that require keyword-stuffed articles, undisclosed paid links, unrelated placements, automated comments, link farms, or bulk directory submissions. A sponsored placement must be labelled and judged for referral value, audience fit, and trust—not only link metrics.
