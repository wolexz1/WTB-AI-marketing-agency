The short answer

What is an AI receptionist for a business?

An AI receptionist, also called an AI call agent or AI voice agent, is a phone assistant designed to answer routine inbound calls using your approved business information. Depending on your phone and software setup, it can ask qualifying questions, capture contact details, check appointment availability, send a call summary and transfer or schedule a callback with a human. It should never guess when it does not know.

If customers call during meetings, after hours or while your team is busy, that first response matters. We build the flow around your real sales process—not a robotic “press one” maze.

Answer and qualify

Give callers clear answers about services, location, opening hours and next steps. Ask only the questions your team needs to help them.

Book or route

With the right calendar and phone integrations, offer an available slot or transfer a high-priority caller to the right person.

Follow up without losing context

Share a useful call summary and trigger an appropriate follow-up or callback, subject to your consent and communication rules.

How we set it up

A call flow that knows when to hand over.

  1. Map the calls: review common questions, call volume, business hours, missed-call points and what counts as a qualified lead.
  2. Define the rules: write approved answers, lead questions, privacy boundaries, escalation triggers and the human handoff path.
  3. Connect the tools: assess your number, telephony provider, calendar, CRM and follow-up channels before promising an integration.
  4. Test real conversations: check noisy lines, accents, interruptions, wrong answers, failed transfers and what happens when a human is unavailable.
  5. Improve the funnel: review answered calls, qualified enquiries, booked appointments and follow-up speed—not just call counts.

No magic button

What you should expect before launch.

We will first confirm whether your current number can forward or connect calls, what a local number costs, which tools can integrate, whether call recording is appropriate, and where caller data will be stored. Your team approves the script and handoff rules. We do not promise that every call will convert, every accent will be understood perfectly, or that an AI agent replaces professional judgement.

Already getting enquiries on WhatsApp too? A coordinated WhatsApp AI workflow can help your team keep the conversation moving after the call, where the customer has agreed to that channel.

Common questions

Before you ask us to build one

Can an AI receptionist answer calls to our existing number?

Possibly. It depends on your current provider and whether call forwarding or an integration is available. We check that before recommending a setup.

Can it book appointments and send follow-ups?

Yes, if the calendar and messaging tools support a reliable integration. We define what it may confirm automatically and when a person must review it.

Can it transfer a serious buyer to a human?

That is part of the design. We set clear triggers for live transfer, a scheduled callback or a message to the right team member when nobody is available.

What do you need from us to scope the project?

Your business number and provider, typical monthly call volume, top caller questions, opening hours, booking process, current CRM or calendar, preferred handoff contacts and budget range.
