Our process

From brief to a campaign you can actually measure.

  1. Brief: audience, offer, brand rules, product truth, budget and channel.
  2. Concept: hook, script, storyboard, voice and visual direction.
  3. Production: AI-assisted footage where suitable, editing, captions, music and human quality checks.
  4. Launch: format variants, landing destination, tracking and creative tests with your media plan.

We check spelling, faces, hands, product details, logo use, pronunciation, claims and usage rights before delivery. We will not invent testimonials, pretend synthetic people are real customers, or promise an ad will go viral.

Before you send the brief

Questions brands ask us

Can AI video replace a full film shoot?

Sometimes it can reduce location, cast or production needs; sometimes real footage is essential for trust, product accuracy or compliance. We recommend the right mix after seeing the brief.

Can you produce Nigerian accents or local scenes?

We can plan locally relevant scripts, voices and visuals, then check them with human review. We do not assume a generated accent or street scene is convincing just because a model produced it.

What do you need to quote a project?

Your product or service, target audience, offer, intended channels, reference style, usage rights, deadline, available product assets and budget range. If you have no brief yet, tell us the business goal.

Will AI video ads guarantee more sales?

No agency can guarantee that. We build clear creative, useful landing paths and testable versions, then judge results against the campaign objective.
