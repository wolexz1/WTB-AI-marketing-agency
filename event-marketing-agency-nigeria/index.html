How we work

From “we have announced” to “we have a route to attendance.”

  1. Build the event proposition. What makes this worth a ticket, a calendar slot and a message to friends?
  2. Create the campaign kit. Ticket-page recommendations, creative directions, key messages, content and a channel plan.
  3. Launch with an early reason to act. Early-bird, speaker or artist reveals, limited tiers, community access or a useful registration incentive.
  4. Learn and improve weekly. We read the response, shift creative or targeting, retarget warm audiences and keep the ticket path clear.
  5. Turn turnout into proof. Capture the story, results and audience signals that help sell the next event, sponsorship or partnership.

Questions

Can you help sell tickets for my event in Nigeria?

Yes. We can connect PR support, creator content, Meta ads, TikTok and Instagram content, Google intent capture, WhatsApp follow-up and retargeting into one campaign direction.
