# WTB AI Overview, GEO, AEO, and SEO Audit

Date: June 9, 2026
Site: https://wtbaimarketing.com/

## What this audit is for

This audit checks how ready WTB is for:

- normal Google ranking
- Google AI Overviews
- Google AI Mode style answer surfaces
- broader generative engine discoverability
- answer-engine optimization for direct question queries

This is not a promise of top placement. It is a readiness audit based on:

- page structure
- trust and entity signals
- answer extraction signals
- schema support
- local relevance
- conversion readiness

## Simple truth first

WTB is already on a good path.

The site already has several of the things Google says matter for AI features:

- crawlable pages
- canonical tags
- robots tags
- strong short-answer sections on many key pages
- FAQ schema on many key pages
- internal linking
- Nigeria, Lagos, and Abuja topical relevance
- structured blog content with current dates and sources

The biggest remaining gap is not raw indexing. It is consistency.

Some pages are already built like answer-engine pages. Others still look more like standard marketing pages and need the same treatment.

## What Google says matters

According to Google's documentation for AI features in Search, there are no special extra requirements just for AI Overviews or AI Mode. The same foundational SEO best practices still apply. Important points include:

- the page must be indexed and eligible to show in Google Search
- important content should be available in text form
- strong internal linking still matters
- structured data should match visible content
- page experience still matters
- Business Profile and Merchant Center freshness matter where relevant

That means the real work is:

- better answers
- stronger trust
- clearer structure
- more original value
- stronger brand/entity signals

- 9 to 10 = very strong
- 7 to 8 = strong, but can improve
- 5 to 6 = fair, needs more support

## What is already in place

### Good foundation already present

- many important pages already have direct short-answer blocks
- blog posts are using FAQ schema and article schema
- location pages are already aligned to Lagos, Abuja, and Nigeria
- canonicals are present
- robots directives are present
- internal CTA and service linking are in place
- several money pages already have breadcrumb schema

### Strong content behaviors already helping

- reader-first voice
- problem-solution framing
- fresh trend coverage
- comparison and pricing content
- local business framing
- practical next steps

These are exactly the kinds of things that make AI systems more comfortable pulling passages or recommending pages.

## Biggest weaknesses holding WTB back

### 1. Trust and entity consistency are not equally strong across the whole site

Some pages feel like answer-engine pages. Others still feel like standard marketing landing pages.

What is missing on weaker pages:

- no short answer near the top
- no FAQ block
- no breadcrumb schema
- weak or missing evidence of editorial review
- weaker explanation of why WTB should be trusted on the topic

### 2. Contact page is under-optimized

The contact page is more important than people think for AI trust and user confidence.

Current issue:

- missing meta description
- no answer block
- no FAQ
- no breadcrumb support

### 3. About page should do more heavy trust work

Right now the About page is useful, but it should more clearly support:

- who WTB helps
- what WTB knows deeply
- how WTB works
- what markets WTB understands
- why Google or AI systems should see the brand as a reliable source

### 4. AI Marketing Agency Abuja page is behind the rest

Compared with Lagos and the stronger service pages, this page needs:

- a clear short answer near the top
- FAQ schema
- breadcrumb schema
- more structured, extractable answers

### 5. Author and review signals are still light on non-blog pages

The blog articles already show stronger author/editor patterns. The sales and trust pages should also signal:

- who produced the guidance
- who reviewed the guidance
- why the page is reliable

## Best fixes in order

### Priority 1: Fix these immediately

1. Add a real meta description to `/contact/`
2. Add short-answer panels to `/about/`, `/contact/`, and `/ai-marketing-agency-abuja/`
3. Add FAQ schema and visible quick answers to `/about/`, `/contact/`, and `/ai-marketing-agency-abuja/`
4. Add breadcrumb schema to those same pages

### Priority 2: Strengthen trust signals

1. Add stronger expertise framing to `/about/`
2. Add reviewed-by or editorial-trust pattern on key service pages
3. Add more proof language around Nigeria, Lagos, Abuja, and category expertise
4. Make the homepage more explicit about what WTB is best known for

### Priority 3: Increase AI Overview extractability

1. Keep direct answers within the first visible section on every money page
2. Use more comparison-style sections
3. Use more "when this is right for you" and "when this is not right for you" sections
4. Add more exact dates and named sources in trend posts

### Priority 4: Improve entity strength beyond the site

1. Verify and optimize the Google Business Profile
2. Build citations and mentions around WTB on relevant platforms
3. Build branded search demand through social, WhatsApp, LinkedIn, and creator mentions
4. Keep publishing original Nigeria-specific marketing analysis instead of generic summaries
