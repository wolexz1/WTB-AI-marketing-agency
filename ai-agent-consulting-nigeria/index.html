Useful beats flashy

Your agent should reduce friction, not create a new problem.

A business agent needs a clear job, trusted knowledge, boundaries, escalation rules, and a way to prove whether it helped. Otherwise you have a clever demo that can answer questions but cannot move the customer or team forward.

WTB connects agent strategy to lead capture, customer service, sales follow-up, internal knowledge, and marketing workflows.

Build it properly

Four decisions make the difference.

Purpose

Give it one clear job

Start with a narrow outcome such as qualification, answers, routing, booking, or follow-up.

Boundaries

Define what it must not do

Set approved knowledge, permissions, privacy rules, tone, and human escalation triggers.

Value

Measure the improvement

Track response time, qualified conversations, resolved requests, booked calls, time saved, and customer feedback.
