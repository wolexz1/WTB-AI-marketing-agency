# WTB Blog Publication Standard

Use this checklist before publishing any new blog on `wtbaimarketing.com`.

## Strategy Check

- Is the topic important right now in Nigeria, Lagos, Abuja, or the wider African market?
- Is it either trending, commercially important, or both?
- Does it connect naturally to a WTB service, offer, workflow, or strategy call?
- Is the angle useful for search engines, AI answer engines, and real human readers?

## Research Check

- Use current, source-backed facts when the article depends on trends, updates, pricing shifts, or platform changes.
- Prefer primary or high-credibility reporting where possible.
- Confirm dates clearly when referencing recent announcements.
- Do not publish generic trend filler.

## Writing Check

- Start with a strong buyer-intent or curiosity-led title.
- Include one H1 only.
- Add a short answer near the top.
- Add Nigerian context early.
- Use clear H2/H3 structure.
- Add useful comparison, mistake, or "best for" sections when relevant.
- Make the writing sound human, practical, sharp, and slightly spicy when it helps.
- Avoid boring corporate filler.

## WTB Relevance Check

- Link to the most relevant WTB service pages.
- End with a clear CTA to `contact`, `website-brief`, `services`, or `strategy call`.
- Make sure the article teaches, builds trust, and quietly sells WTB.

## Visual Check

- Use human-led visuals, not random shapes alone.
- Show a person, customer, founder, marketer, or business moment connected to the topic.
- Make the image feel story-driven and emotionally relevant.
- Make sure the visual clearly relates to the exact content of the blog.
- Avoid awkward crops, cut-off heads, or compositions that hide the human subject.
- For blog listing cards, prefer full-scene human images that still read clearly at smaller sizes.
- Use strong alt text.

## Technical SEO Check

- Meta description between 50 and 160 characters.
- Valid canonical URL.
- Valid JSON-LD.
- Good image alt text.
- No duplicate URL variants in sitemap.
- Add `_redirects` and `_headers` entries for `/index.html`.
- Update `sitemap.xml`, `llms.txt`, and `markdown-mirror.md`.

## Final Quality Check

- Read the article like a real Nigerian business owner.
- Ask: "Would this feel useful, current, credible, and worth sharing?"
- If it sounds generic, rewrite it before publishing.
