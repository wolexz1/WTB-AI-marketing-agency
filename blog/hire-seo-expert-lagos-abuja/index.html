SEO hiring guide

How to hire an SEO expert in Lagos or Abuja without wasting money.

By WTB Team | Updated May 27, 2026

If your business needs leads from Google, the right SEO expert should give you clearer pages, better keyword targeting, stronger local signals, and reporting you can understand.

Discuss this with WTBView services
Short answer

Hire an SEO expert when your website is live but not bringing enough qualified search traffic. A good SEO expert in Lagos or Abuja should audit your pages, map buyer-intent keywords, improve your titles and content, fix indexing issues, and show you what to build next.

What should an SEO expert do first?

The first job is not to promise page-one rankings. The first job is diagnosis. Your SEO expert should check your website structure, Search Console, sitemap, page titles, descriptions, headings, internal links, local keywords, competitor pages, and conversion paths.

What to ask before you pay

  • Which keywords can bring buying intent, not just random traffic?
  • What pages are missing from the website?
  • What technical issues could stop Google from indexing the site?
  • How will success be reported every month?
  • Will the SEO plan include local terms for Lagos, Abuja, and Nigeria?

Warning signs

Be careful with anyone who guarantees instant ranking, cannot explain what will be changed, ignores Search Console, sells backlinks as the whole strategy, or gives you traffic reports without leads, calls, form submissions, or business context.
