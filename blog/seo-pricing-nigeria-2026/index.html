SEO pricing in Nigeria: how much should a serious business budget?

By WTB Team | Updated May 27, 2026

SEO pricing should be tied to the work needed to make your website easier to find, understand, trust, and convert.

Discuss this with WTBView services
Short answer

In Nigeria, WTB SEO audit and setup starts from NGN 160,000. Monthly SEO growth can start from NGN 250,000, while competitive SEO for ecommerce, real estate, education, skincare, food, and professional services can start from NGN 450,000 per month depending on scope.

Why SEO prices are different from one business to another

A five-page service website does not need the same SEO work as an ecommerce store with many product pages. Pricing changes based on competition, location targets, website size, technical problems, content gaps, reporting needs, and how many pages must be created or improved.

What a proper SEO budget should cover

  • Keyword research focused on buyer intent.
  • Page titles, meta descriptions, headings, and internal links.
  • Local SEO signals for Lagos, Abuja, and Nigeria searches.
  • Search Console checks, sitemap review, and indexing signals.
  • Content planning for service pages, blog posts, and FAQs.
  • Monthly reporting that explains what changed and what to do next.

When a cheap SEO package is risky

Cheap SEO becomes expensive when it only gives reports without improving pages, fixing indexing issues, or building content that matches search intent. A good SEO plan should show what pages will rank, what keywords they target, and how visitors will turn into leads.
