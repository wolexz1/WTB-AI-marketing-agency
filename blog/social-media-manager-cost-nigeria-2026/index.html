How much do social media managers charge in Nigeria?

By WTB Team | Updated May 27, 2026

Social media pricing depends on whether you need posting only, strategy, creative production, paid ads, influencer support, or a full marketing system.

Discuss this with WTBView services
Short answer

In Nigeria, basic social media management can cost less than a full campaign system, but if you want serious support, you should budget for strategy, content planning, captions, creative direction, ads support, reporting, and optimization. WTB campaign execution starts from NGN 550,000 per month when social media is part of a broader marketing system.

Before choosing a provider, decide whether your business needs posting, content production, community management, paid social, creator support, or a complete growth system. Compare the wider social media management pricing and WTB services pages.

What changes the price?

  • Number of platforms managed.
  • How many posts, reels, carousels, or videos are needed each month.
  • Whether the agency is creating content or only posting supplied assets.
  • Paid social ads management and retargeting.
  • Influencer, mini UGC, or creator campaign coordination.
  • Reporting, campaign testing, and weekly optimization.

Posting is not the same as marketing

A low-cost manager may help a page stay active. A stronger social media marketer helps your business connect content to offers, audiences, campaigns, traffic, leads, and sales. This is why the scope matters more than the title.

What WTB includes in social media support

WTB can handle content calendars, captions, campaign angles, Reels ideas, carousels, creator briefs, influencer coordination, paid social ads, reporting, and AI-assisted creative production.

Who needs a full social media system?

Ecommerce brands, skincare brands, food brands, education brands, real estate teams, creators, and service businesses usually need more than posting. They need repeatable content, campaign proof, ad learning, and clear customer action.
