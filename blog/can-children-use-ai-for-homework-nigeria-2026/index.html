Can children use AI for homework? A parent guide to helpful use versus cheating.

By WTB Team | Updated July 23, 2026

AI can explain, quiz and help a child get unstuck. It should not quietly become the child who does the homework.

Explore AI Explorers for ages 9-11
Short answer

AI is most useful when it helps a child understand a task, practise a skill or check their thinking. It becomes harmful when it produces work the child cannot explain.

Use AI before, during and after learning - not instead of it

Before a task, it can help turn a hard topic into simpler language. During a task, it can suggest practice questions. Afterwards, it can help the child compare their answer with another explanation. The child should still do the reasoning.

Three healthy homework prompts

  • Explain this topic in a way a 10-year-old can understand.
  • Give me five practice questions, but do not show the answers yet.
  • Ask me questions so I can work out what I missed.

Three warning signs

Step in if your child copies an answer they cannot explain, hides the tool use, or starts asking AI to complete every task. The correction is not shame. It is a return to process, explanation and honest effort.

A simple family rule

If your child cannot explain the final answer in their own words, it is not ready to submit.
