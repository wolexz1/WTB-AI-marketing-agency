How much does website design cost in Nigeria in 2026?

By WTB Team | Updated May 27, 2026

The real cost depends on what the website must do: inform, collect leads, sell products, rank on Google, or connect to systems.

Discuss this with WTBView services
Short answer

A basic business website in Nigeria can start from NGN 150,000. Extra needs like SEO setup, ecommerce, login dashboards, copywriting, custom domain payment, email setup, and advanced automation increase the project cost.

What affects website pricing?

  • Number of pages and sections
  • Whether you already have logo, colours, photos, and copy
  • SEO setup and keyword landing pages
  • Ecommerce products, payment setup, delivery details, and order flow
  • Login areas, dashboards, booking systems, or custom forms
  • Domain, hosting, business email, and analytics setup

Why cheap websites can become expensive later

A website that looks fine but has weak structure can cost you leads. Poor mobile layout, unclear copy, slow pages, missing tracking, and bad SEO setup make it harder for customers and search engines to trust the business.
