AI rules for kids: a simple family safety checklist.

By WTB Team | Updated July 23, 2026

A family does not need twenty complicated AI rules. It needs a few clear ones that a child can remember and use.

Explore AI Explorers for ages 9-11
Short answer

The strongest family AI rules cover privacy, adult guidance, verification, honest schoolwork and the child's own thinking.

The five rules to put where your child can see them

  1. I do not share private family information.
  2. I ask an adult before trying a new AI tool.
  3. I check important answers.
  4. I use AI to help me learn, not to pretend I did the work.
  5. I stop and speak to an adult if anything feels strange, upsetting or confusing.

Turn rules into a conversation

Ask your child to explain each rule in their own example. The goal is understanding, not recitation.
