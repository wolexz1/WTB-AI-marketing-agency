AI workbook for kids: what parents should look for before they buy.

By WTB Team | Updated July 23, 2026

A good AI workbook should not just make technology look exciting. It should help a child think more carefully around it.

Explore AI Explorers for ages 9-11
Short answer

Look for a workbook that is age-appropriate, parent-guided, practical, privacy-aware and designed to strengthen a child's own judgement.

Check age fit before design

Children need language, examples and activities that meet them where they are. A tool made for older teens is not automatically suitable for a nine-year-old.

Look for active learning

Better workbooks ask children to compare, explain, decide and create. Passive reading or flashy tool lists do not build much confidence.

Safety should not be an appendix

Privacy, misinformation, honesty and adult guidance should be built into the activities, not added as a small warning at the end.

Make sure a parent can participate

The most useful product gives an adult a clear role even if the adult does not know much about AI yet.
