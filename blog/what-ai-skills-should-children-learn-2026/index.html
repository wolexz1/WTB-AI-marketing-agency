AI literacy for Nigerian families

What AI skills should children learn first? A simple 2026 roadmap for parents.

By WTB Team | Updated July 23, 2026

The first AI skill is not prompt writing. It is judgement: knowing when to ask, when to pause and when to check.

Explore AI Explorers for ages 9-11
Short answer

Children benefit most from learning critical thinking, privacy, fact-checking and responsible creativity before they chase advanced AI tools.

The six skills that matter first

  1. Pattern awareness: notice where AI already appears.
  2. Better questions: learn that clear questions shape useful answers.
  3. Verification: separate a confident answer from a reliable one.
  4. Privacy: know what must never be typed into a tool.
  5. Original thinking: use suggestions without losing their own voice.
  6. Kindness and boundaries: use technology without cheating, bullying or overdependence.

What can wait

Children do not need to learn complex automation, coding tools or every new AI platform to become AI-literate. A solid foundation is more valuable than a long tool list.

How parents can help

Choose one small activity each week: check an answer, improve a question, make a quiz, or talk about privacy. Small habits build real confidence.
