App launch strategy

How to market an app in Nigeria without shouting into the void.

Launching an app can feel like planning a party and praying people do not ignore the invite. The truth is, good app marketing is less about volume and more about fit: the right message, for the right people, in the right places, with a smoother path after the install. WTB helps your business build that path.

Book a strategy call
Short answer

To market an app in Nigeria well, your business needs clearer category messaging, better launch sequencing, stronger app store visibility, smarter channel choices, and a more convincing reason for users to care now.

What people miss

App marketing starts before launch day, not after it.

Many teams wait until the app is ready, then suddenly begin asking how to get users. But the stronger question is what the market needs to understand before the app goes live, during launch week, and in the weeks after the first spike of attention fades.

WTB helps your business plan the message, the landing path, the creator or community angle, the paid support, the content sequence, and the conversion logic that gives the app a better chance of sticking in people’s minds.
