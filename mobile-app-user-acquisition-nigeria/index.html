Short answer

Mobile app user acquisition should help your business bring in users that are more likely to activate, stay, and become valuable, not just inflate install counts for screenshots and investor updates.

The real problem

Many apps are not struggling with attention. They are struggling with fit, funnel, and follow-through.

Your business may already be running ads, posting content, speaking to creators, or pushing referral loops. But if the audience does not quickly understand the app, trust the promise, and move smoothly through onboarding, growth gets expensive fast.

WTB helps your business tighten the acquisition message, improve creative direction, align channels to the right audience, and connect install campaigns to better onboarding and retention logic.
