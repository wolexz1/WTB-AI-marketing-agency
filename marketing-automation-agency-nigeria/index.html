Short answer

A marketing automation agency in Nigeria helps your business respond faster, route leads better, recover missed opportunities, and keep follow-up working even when the team is busy.

What breaks growth

Many businesses do not need more leads first. They need fewer leaks.

Someone clicks an ad, fills a form, sends a message, or asks for pricing, and the next step depends on memory, manual replies, or a scattered team handoff. That is where momentum dies.

Good automation protects momentum. It helps your business respond while interest is still fresh, keeps follow-up consistent, and makes campaign performance easier to understand.

What we build

Practical automation around the full customer journey.

We keep the system grounded in how people discover, ask, hesitate, decide, and return, not just in what the tool can technically automate.

Routing

Lead capture and routing

Forms, landing pages, qualification steps, WhatsApp handoff, internal alerts, and the logic that sends each enquiry to the right place.

Follow-up

Nurture and reminder flows

Sequences for new leads, no-shows, abandoned interest, unclosed enquiries, and customers who need the next push.

Recovery

Lost-opportunity recovery

Abandoned cart reminders, delayed replies, stale prospects, and inactive customers who may still be worth winning back.

Retargeting

Ad and audience continuation

Logic that helps interested visitors see a more relevant next message after clicking, viewing, or engaging.

Visibility

Reporting and tracking

Tracking setup that shows where leads came from, where they dropped, and what follow-up pattern deserves more budget.

Integration

Cross-channel coordination

Email, SMS, WhatsApp, ads, landing pages, and simple CRM-style workflows working together instead of separately.
