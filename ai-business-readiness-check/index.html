Straight answers

Before you add anything to the plan

Does this tool inspect my website automatically?

No. This is a self-check of your current customer journey, not a live crawler. A real website and AI-search audit needs access to the public site, Search Console and your business goals. We will not label a site “AI-ready” based on a guessed score.

Do I need AI everywhere?

No. Sometimes a clearer page, faster human reply or better ownership solves the problem. We recommend automation only where it improves the customer experience and can be tested.

Why is there no fixed price for each item?

A WhatsApp flow for one product is different from a multi-branch setup connected to stock, payments and a CRM. Phone and messaging provider costs also vary. The check is free; after reviewing your chosen areas, we can give you one scoped, itemised proposal and a practical order of work.

Can I choose several improvements at once?

Yes. Select as many as you want, review the list, then send one combined WhatsApp message or proposal request. You can remove items before sending.
