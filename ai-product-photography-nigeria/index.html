The short answer

What is AI product photography for a brand?

It uses real product references, art direction and AI-assisted editing or scene generation to create useful marketing images. We can develop cleaner product presentations, contextual lifestyle scenes, campaign key visuals and image-ad variants. We do not replace a genuine product with an invented version and call it a photograph. If your packaging, colour, fit or texture must be exact, a real reference shoot is the starting point.

Common questions

Before you commission product visuals

Do I need to send a real product photo?

Usually yes. A clear reference is how we protect your product's identity and details. If the available photos are poor, we will recommend a simple reference shoot first.

Is this different from AI video ads?

Yes. This page focuses on still product imagery and static ad creative. Our AI video service covers scripts, motion, editing and channel-specific video variants.

Can you run the ads as well?

Yes, where that is in the agreed scope. We can connect visuals to audience targeting, landing destinations and reporting rather than handing over files with no distribution plan.

Will the images guarantee sales?

No. Better creative can improve the test, but pricing, offer, landing experience, targeting and follow-up also affect conversion.

Ready to show the product properly?

Send us one product and the buyer you want to reach.

Include a product photo, your store or social link, the campaign goal, deadline, channels and budget. We will suggest a visual direction and the assets worth making first.
