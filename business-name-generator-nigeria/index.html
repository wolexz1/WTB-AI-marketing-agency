Before you fall in love with a name

These are suggestions, not legal advice or live checks. In Nigeria, search the Corporate Affairs Commission portal; elsewhere, consult your country's business registry. Investigate trademarks, and check domain names and social handles independently before using or registering a name. Similar names may already exist.

How do I choose a good business name?

Choose a name your customers can understand, say and remember. Test it with people you want to serve, consider whether it still fits as your business grows, and do the relevant availability checks.
