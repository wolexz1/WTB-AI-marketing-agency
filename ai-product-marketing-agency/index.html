The real challenge

Your product is not “an AI tool.” It is a new job your buyer needs done.

Buyers do not wake up hoping to purchase “more AI.” They want a sales team to stop losing leads, a support desk to answer faster, a creator to finish work in less time, or an operations team to remove a painful manual step.

That is why an excellent model, elegant API, or impressive workflow can still struggle commercially. If the story starts with architecture instead of the user’s outcome, your demo becomes a lecture and your landing page becomes homework.

WTB helps your business make the product easier to explain, easier to compare, and easier to choose—without flattening the technical depth that makes it special.

How the work moves

From “we built something clever” to “the right people know why they need it.”

1. Find the commercial wedge. We identify the sharpest use case, user, moment of pain, and reason to change.

2. Build the buyer story. We make your positioning, product pages, demo flow, proof, and objections work together.

3. Put distribution behind the story. We turn the strongest angles into content, search opportunities, paid testing, PR, partner conversations, and launch activity.

4. Learn and compound. We use enquiry, demo, activation, and campaign signals to improve the message rather than simply adding more noise.

Quick answers

What AI product teams usually want to know first.

Scope

Can WTB market an AI app, agent, API, MCP tool, or SaaS product?

Yes. The work begins with the product’s real use case and buyer, then connects positioning, product storytelling, proof, launch activity, and demand generation.

Timing

Do we need a finished product before go-to-market?

No. A credible prototype, pilot, or defined product direction can be enough to test the buyer story, collect early signals, and prepare a focused launch path.

Difference
